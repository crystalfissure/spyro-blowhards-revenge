# Snow Gnorc — Ice Cavern

Place `/Game/OT_Ports/S1/S1_Enemies/Home_01_Peace_Keepers/03_IceCavern/Snow_Gnorc_BP` in a level. This is the large punching Snow Gnorc, Spyro 1 model class 198. The existing Magic Crafters Ice Gnorc is a different enemy.

`SnowGnorcBehavior` owns animation, facing, combat and reset. It finds the local Spyro automatically; `Pursuer` permits an instance target override. No navigation mesh or patrol route is required. The Blueprint retains the project's shared damageable, item dropper, level gem counting and checkpoint contracts. It has one hit point, resists ordinary charge, and drops one green gem when flamed.

The original NTSC Ice Cavern overlay was compared with the supplied disc: all 8,776 instruction words matched. Class 198's model headers supplied the ten animation lengths/rates, eleven sound samples and pitch settings, scale exponent, sound frame cues and punch collision window. Reference code is pinned to [spyro-1 revision 46b51d3](https://github.com/TheMobyCollective/spyro-1/tree/46b51d3a5c34dd2585b4fe1de74ac9b74c76390d).

The simulation advances at 30 Hz independently of rendering. The Gnorc stays at his spawn point while watching Spyro, raises his fists inside 7,168 original units, punches inside 3,300 units, and lowers his guard beyond 8,192 units. Starting an alert or punch requires a feet-height difference below 1,500 units. Idle/guard facing turns by at most three original angle units per tick; punching permits six. Idle animation 0 alternates with the original 8/9 sequence after randomized idle cycles. A connected punch leads to three cycles of recovery animation 7; a miss returns to idle. Death animation 6 retains the original initial slide speed of 240 units/tick and decay of 16 units/tick.

Punch contact uses class 198 collision group 1, active on animation 4 frames 7–18. Positive interpolation progress selects the next original frame, matching `func_800529E4`. Its type-1 sphere has radius 3,200 original units and zero vertical offset. The Unreal query uses Spyro's capsule radius and the enemy's feet origin; physical walls block contact. Successful player damage is limited to once per punch. Original animation frames are sampled through a native animation instance, rather than playing the FBX's export frame rate directly.

Unreal adaptations: solid/flame contact uses an animated fitted box, player damage uses the existing Crush reaction to flatten Spyro, and death movement sweeps against Unreal terrain. A half-scale `P_DustCloud` puff appears when death animation 6 finishes and the corpse disappears; checkpoint reset removes any lingering puff. `DeathSmoke` permits a Blueprint effect override. Frozen/fear/reset states suspend simulation; paused worlds cannot apply punch damage from pose callbacks. Flame maps to the project's Burn damage type. The original additional `0x80000` defeat flag has no separately implemented mapping here; ordinary Ram remains resisted. Sound attenuation follows the project's Town Square settings.

The component exposes `AttackCommitted` and `ResetCompleted` through `OnEnemySignal`. AttackCommitted reports clip 4 at wind-up; it is not a landed-hit notification and should not apply additional damage. Debug properties show state, original clip/frame, simulation ticks, punch/hit counts, gem count and sound cue history.

The headless fixture restores global game time after damage because it does not provide the normal player controller; it also logs existing missing-controller errors in the player's swimming animation graph.
